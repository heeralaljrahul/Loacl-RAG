# Travel Policy

## Booking flights

Economy is the default for flights under six hours. Premium economy is permitted above six hours; business class needs VP sign-off.

## Hotels

The nightly cap is 180 EUR in most cities and 260 EUR in London, New York, Zurich and Tokyo. Above the cap, attach a written justification.

## Ground transport

Trains are preferred where the journey is under four hours door to door. Taxis are reimbursed; car hire needs a reason recorded.

## Visas

The travel desk handles visa applications and needs at least 20 working days' notice.
