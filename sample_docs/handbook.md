# Acme Employee Handbook 2026

## Annual leave

Full-time staff accrue 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accruing monthly from the start date. Part-time staff accrue pro rata.

### Carry-over

Up to five days may be carried into the following year. Anything beyond five days lapses on 31 March and is not paid out on departure.

### Booking

Requests go through Workday and need line-manager approval at least ten working days in advance for absences longer than three days.

## Expenses

Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the spend. The domestic per-diem rate is 45 EUR; international travel is 78 EUR. Anything above 500 EUR needs pre-approval from a director.

## Remote work

Staff may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements require director approval and a signed contract addendum. The office is closed on the last Friday of August.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks at full pay. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks. Both must be taken within 18 months of the birth or placement.
