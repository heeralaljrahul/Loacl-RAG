# New Starter Onboarding

## First day

IT issues the laptop at 09:30. Building access cards come from reception and need a photograph taken on site.

## Accounts

Single sign-on is provisioned automatically from the HR record overnight. If SSO is missing on day one, raise a ticket rather than emailing IT directly.

## Buddy scheme

Every new starter is assigned a buddy for the first six weeks. The buddy is not the line manager, deliberately.

## Probation

Probation lasts three months and ends with a written review. Extensions are possible once, for a maximum of a further three months.
